Haven Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Haven (“the App”) is a privacy-focused Android application that helps you block apps and websites on your device. This policy explains what Haven does and does not do with your information.

Summary

  • Haven runs locally on your device.
  • Haven does not create an account.
  • Haven does not upload browsing history, passwords, or blocked content to a server.
  • Haven uses Android’s VpnService to run a local, on-device DNS filter. Only website name lookups (DNS) pass through it; blocked names are answered on the device. Allowed lookups are forwarded to a public DNS resolver (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1, or 1.1.1.3 when adult protection is on) or your network’s DNS. All other traffic goes directly to the internet and is never routed through Haven or any Haven server.

Data we collect

Haven does not collect personal data for analytics, advertising, or cloud storage. Settings, block lists, and related rules are stored on your device (for example in local SQLite storage).

Permissions and why they are used

  • Accessibility service — to detect when a blocked app is opened and to read website addresses shown in supported browser address bars so Haven can show a blocking screen. Haven does not use Accessibility to read passwords or to upload browsing history.
  • Device Admin (optional) — to add an extra confirmation step before uninstalling Haven while protection is enabled. You can disable this anytime in system settings.
  • Query installed apps — so you can choose which apps to block from a list of apps installed on your device.
  • Notifications (optional) — to show protection-related status when you allow it.
  • Boot completed — to restore protection after reboot when you previously left protection enabled.

Internet access

The App may request network access for normal Android / Flutter platform behavior (for example loading fonts). Haven does not send your browsing history or block-list activity to Haven servers, because Haven does not operate a user-data backend.

Children

Haven is not directed at children under 13. If you use Haven for family safety, the device owner / parent remains responsible for configuration and supervision.

Changes

We may update this policy when the App changes. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
